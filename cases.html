Case Study 01 · Primary Machine Learning Lane

Content Refresh Opportunity Scoring

Beat 1: The Problem

Content marketing teams waste ~4.0 hours per article manually rewriting decaying posts based on arbitrary staleness rules. Traditional static heuristics flag old pages regardless of traffic impact, leading to a high rate of false positive effort on zero-traffic URLs.

Beat 2: What I Did & Decided

I built an exploratory Random Forest priority-scoring prototype using a 30,000-row anonymized snapshot across 32 clients. A later audit found that 90-day feature aggregates overlap the recent 30-day target period. Excluding explicit target-derived fields and grouping clients does not remove this temporal leakage risk.

Beat 3: What Came of It

The original Precision@K and ROC-AUC figures have been withdrawn as predictive-performance claims. The prototype demonstrates a data-preparation, model-experimentation, and decision-support workflow; a valid evaluation requires point-in-time features and a forward outcome window.

Validation status: exploratory only · no approved predictive metrics
Case Study 02 · Signal Verification

Search Rank Volatility & Position Tier Audit

Beat 1: The Problem

SEO teams frequently confuse normal rank fluctuation in top positions with active search traffic decay, leading to premature title tag changes on healthy URLs.

Beat 2: What I Did & Decided

I conducted a signal audit across Google position tiers (Top 3 vs Pos 4-10 vs Pos 11-20), computing decay rates with explicit sample sizes (n). I confirmed that pages in position 4-10 with low CTR represent the highest-value optimization targets.

Beat 3: What Came of It

Defined a transparent rule baseline yielding +77.3 percentage point lift over random base rate selection.

Audited Dataset: 28,795 Ranked URLs
