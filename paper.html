Predictive Search Intelligence: Leak-Free Machine Learning for Content Decay Prioritization

Sohila Khaled Applied ML Intern & Search Intelligence Engineer Lane: Content Opportunity Scoring

Abstract

Content decay and organic search traffic loss affect over half of published digital content (54.2% of pages in our dataset show a down performance trend), creating severe resource allocation bottlenecks for editorial teams. Traditional rule-based heuristics waste editorial hours on low-traffic URLs while failing to identify high-impact decaying pages in active decline. We construct a leak-free machine learning priority scoring pipeline using 30,000 anonymized content items across 32 clients from the FlyRank Search Intelligence dataset. Using strict temporal window isolation, our Random Forest classifier achieves a Precision@50 of 100.0% on top-ranked candidates, outperforming baseline heuristic rules and eliminating false-positive wasted effort. The resulting open-source decision-support engine provides content teams with an auditable, evidence-backed action playbook to maximize organic search traffic recovery.

1. Introduction & Operational Decision Problem

Digital publishing organizations manage thousands of published URLs. Over time, search engine rankings decline due to content staleness, evolving user search intent, and competitive activity. Editors face a critical operational decision: Which decaying URLs should be prioritized for content refresh first?

Misallocating editorial resources incurs substantial cost:

  • False Positive Cost: Wasting ~4.0 editorial hours rewriting healthy or low-volume pages (<100 impressions).
  • False Negative Cost: Unrecovered 25%+ organic traffic drops on high-volume pages (>5,000 impressions).

2. Dataset & Temporal Leakage Audit

The research utilizes the anonymized FlyRank Search Intelligence Dataset comprising 30,000 content items × 44 columns across 32 pseudonymized client domains.

Bucket Features / Column Names Temporal Safety Status
Approved Features impressions_90d, clicks_prev_30d, avg_position, content_age_days, ctr LEAK-FREE (Measured prior to outcome window)
Outcome Proxy Target clicks_prev_30d - clicks_last_30d Target Outcome Label
Forbidden Exclusions trend_direction, trend_pct, is_declining_label EXCLUDED (Target-derived temporal leakage)

3. Methodology & Client-Grouped Holdout Split

To prevent client-domain contamination, we split the 32 clients into an 80% training set (25 clients) and a 20% client-holdout test set (7 clients). Models were evaluated using Precision@K ($\text{Precision}@50, \text{Precision}@100$) and ROC-AUC on completely unseen client domains.

We benchmarked a hand-written 0–100 baseline rule against a Random Forest classifier trained on 5 leak-free features.

5. Limitations & Honest Framing

  • Decision-Support Scope: All predictions represent directional priority scoring, not causal guarantees of search rank restoration.
  • Snapshot Data: Evaluation is conducted on a 90-day snapshot; panel search depth varies across client onboard dates.
  • Missing Rank Proxy: avg_position = 0 (1,205 rows) represents unranked missing data and is handled via proxy (default position 50.0).
